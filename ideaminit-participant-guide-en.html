A code-protected board may ask for the code again after a reload or in a new tab. If the board is temporarily unavailable, not found, or has been moved to Trash, contact the organiser rather than creating an account.
You have been invited to share ideas on an IdeaMinit board. This guide explains everything you need to participate — it takes about two minutes to read.

Accessing the board

1
Open the link your organiser shared with you

The board works in any modern browser — no app or account needed.

2
Enter the access code if prompted

Some boards are protected. Your organiser will give you the code. It is typically a short word or phrase (e.g. TEAM2025).

3
You will see the board with its lanes (columns)

Each lane is a category. Ideas are posted into a specific lane.

Browsing and sorting ideas

Use the Sort menu in the board header to show the oldest ideas first, newest ideas first, or ideas with the most votes first. Sorting only changes how ideas are displayed; it does not move them between lanes.

On smaller screens, select a lane tab to view that category. Use Load more when a lane contains additional ideas.

The organiser sets the lane order; it is shared across desktop columns and mobile tabs. Use the information icon beside the board title to read the organiser's description. Long idea descriptions have a Read more control. Ideas, counts, and lanes refresh periodically while the board is open; reload if board details appear out of date.

Sharing an idea

1
Click the main button (e.g. "Add Idea")

The button is in the top area of the board. Its label may be customised by your organiser.

2
Fill in the form
  • Title (required) — a short summary of your idea
  • Details (optional) — extra context or explanation
  • Lane (required) — the category your idea belongs in
  • Your name (optional) — leave blank to post anonymously
3
Click Post to board — your idea appears after it is saved
ℹ️ Anonymity Your name is free text and is not verified. Leaving it blank posts as "Anonymous". Your organiser can see whatever name you entered, but no account or identity is required to participate.

Participants cannot edit, move, or delete posted ideas or delete comments. Ask the board manager to correct or remove content. If no lanes exist yet, ask the organiser to add one before posting.

Voting

You can vote on ideas to show support. There is no limit to how many ideas you can vote for.

1
Click the vote button on an idea card to support it

The vote count increases immediately.

2
Click again to remove your vote

You can vote and un-vote freely while the board is open.

ℹ️ How votes are tracked Votes are linked to a signed browser identity stored in a long-lived cookie, not to a user account. There is one vote per idea per browser identity. A different browser or device receives a different identity, and clearing site data may reset it.

Commenting

1
Click "Show comments" on an idea card
2
Optionally enter your name, then type your comment

Leave the name blank to comment anonymously.

3
Press Enter or click the send button

Use Shift+Enter for a new line without sending.

When the board is locked

An amber banner means the board is read-only. This may be a temporary pause or the end of collection. You can still read ideas, vote totals, and existing comments, but cannot add ideas, vote, undo votes, or add comments. Archived boards are also read-only. A site put on hold or a board moved to Trash is unavailable instead. Contact your organiser if this is unexpected.
